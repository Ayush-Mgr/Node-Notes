This is the paper most likely to hurt you.
Focus only on:
Stationarity
ACF vs PACF
AR, MA, ARIMA
Differencing
Forecasting methods
Trend and Seasonality
These topics cover a huge chunk of the syllabus.

Hadoop
HDFS
MapReduce
Spark
DataFrames vs RDD
Data Cleaning
EDA
Those are likely question generators.

Principles
Types of charts
Storytelling
