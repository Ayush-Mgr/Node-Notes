## Normalization
 - it is used to scale and standardize the feature
 - goal is to bring in a standard scale
 - eg , all the texts and words are converted and stored in a standard binary format in the computer
  types:
  - min - max normalization 
  - z-score normalization

## min max
$$
X_{\text{norm}}
=
\frac{X - X_{\min}}
     {X_{\max} - X_{\min}}
$$

it brings the entire data under the scale of 0 and  1
