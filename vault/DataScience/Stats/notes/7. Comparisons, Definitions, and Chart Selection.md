# Comparisons, Definitions, and Chart Selection Questions

---

# Part 1: Important Definitions

### 1. Data Visualization

The graphical representation of data using charts, graphs, maps, and dashboards to make information easier to understand.

---

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The process of analyzing and visualizing data to discover patterns, trends, relationships, and outliers before applying statistical or machine learning models.

---

### 3. Visual Analytics

The combination of visualization, analytics, and interactive techniques to analyze complex datasets.

---

### 4. Business Intelligence (BI)

The process of collecting, analyzing, and visualizing business data to support better decision-making.

---

### 5. Scientific Visualization

The graphical representation of scientific and engineering data using 2D or 3D visualizations.

---

### 6. Storytelling with Data

Using data, visualizations, and narrative together to communicate insights effectively.

---

### 7. Human Perception

The way people interpret visual information such as position, size, color, and shape.

---

### 8. Color Theory

The use of colors to represent information effectively and improve readability.

---

### 9. Dashboard

A collection of multiple visualizations that provides a summary of key performance indicators (KPIs).

---

### 10. Interactive Visualization

Visualization that allows users to interact using filters, zoom, drill-down, and tooltips.

---

# Part 2: Important Comparisons

## 1. Categorical vs Numerical Data

|Categorical|Numerical|
|---|---|
|Qualitative|Quantitative|
|Represents labels|Represents numbers|
|Cannot perform arithmetic|Arithmetic operations possible|
|Example: Gender|Example: Age|
|Bar/Pie Charts|Histogram/Scatter Plot|

---

## 2. Discrete vs Continuous Data

|Discrete|Continuous|
|---|---|
|Countable values|Measurable values|
|Whole numbers|Decimal values|
|Example: Students|Example: Height|
|Bar Chart|Histogram|

---

## 3. Tableau vs Power BI vs D3.js

|Tableau|Power BI|D3.js|
|---|---|---|
|BI Tool|BI Tool|JavaScript Library|
|Drag-and-drop|Drag-and-drop|Coding required|
|Paid|Free & Paid|Free|
|Easy|Moderate|Difficult|
|Best for dashboards|Microsoft ecosystem|Custom web visualizations|

---

## 4. Good Chart vs Bad Chart

|Good Chart|Bad Chart|
|---|---|
|Simple|Cluttered|
|Clear labels|Missing labels|
|Proper colors|Too many colors|
|Correct chart|Wrong chart|
|Honest scale|Misleading scale|

---

## 5. Static vs Interactive Visualization

|Static|Interactive|
|---|---|
|No interaction|Filters, Zoom, Drill-down|
|Printed reports|Dashboards|
|Fixed view|Dynamic exploration|

---

## 6. Time Series vs Categorical Charts

|Time Series|Categorical|
|---|---|
|Shows trends over time|Compares categories|
|X-axis = Time|X-axis = Categories|
|Line Chart|Bar Chart|

---

## 7. EDA vs BI

|EDA|BI|
|---|---|
|Explore data|Business decisions|
|Data scientists|Managers & executives|
|Before modeling|Business reporting|
|Python, R|Power BI, Tableau|

---

## 8. Visual Analytics vs Scientific Visualization

|Visual Analytics|Scientific Visualization|
|---|---|
|Business & analytics|Scientific data|
|Interactive dashboards|2D & 3D simulations|
|Decision support|Research & engineering|

|---|---|
|Compare categories|Bar Chart|

|Show percentage or parts of a whole|Pie Chart|
|Show frequency distribution|Histogram|
|Show relationship between two variables|Scatter Plot|
|Detect outliers|Box Plot|
|Compare three or more variables|Bubble Chart|
|Show correlations|Heat Map|
|Show parent-child hierarchy|Tree Map|
|Show network relationships|Network Graph|
|Show geographical information|Choropleth Map|
|Show rankings|Horizontal Bar Chart|
|Show cumulative totals|Area Chart|
|Show stock prices|Candlestick Chart|
|Show project timeline|Gantt Chart|
|Show organizational structure|Tree Diagram|
|Show clusters|Dendrogram|
|Show flow between categories|Sankey Diagram|

# Frequently Asked Exam Questions

### 1. Which chart is best for comparing sales of products?

**Answer:** Bar Chart

### 2. Which chart is used for stock market analysis?

**Answer:** Candlestick Chart

### 3. Which chart is best for showing trends over time?

**Answer:** Line Chart

### 4. Which chart shows the relationship between two numerical variables?

**Answer:** Scatter Plot

### 5. Which chart is used to detect outliers?

**Answer:** Box Plot

### 6. Which chart is suitable for hierarchical data?

**Answer:** Tree Map or Tree Diagram

### 7. Which visualization is used for geographic data?

**Answer:** Choropleth Map or Symbol Map

### 8. Which chart displays data distribution?

**Answer:** Histogram

### 9. Which chart represents percentages of a whole?

**Answer:** Pie Chart

### 10. Which chart is suitable for correlation analysis?

**Answer:** Heat Map or Scatter Plot

- **T** – Trend → **Line Chart**

- **D** – Distribution → **Histogram**
