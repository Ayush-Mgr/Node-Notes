## K-means clustering

K = no. of custers

steps 
1. state no of clusters k = 3
2. select random cluster toal =k , random 3 data  points  initial data points
3. measure the [[Euclidean distance|distance]] of first point to all 3 random data points 
4. assign the first data point to the nearest selected  data point 
5. do  same 3 and 4 step to all others , forming clusters at each selected points 
6. calculate the mean of each clusters
7. measure the distance of first point to mean of each clusters 
8. assign the first data point to the nearest mean of the selected cluster
9. evaluate the variation of clusters 
10. repeate the 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9 , till the optimal low variation cluster is found selected 

how to find optimal no. of  clusters (K)
1. try all and pick best when varriationis low
