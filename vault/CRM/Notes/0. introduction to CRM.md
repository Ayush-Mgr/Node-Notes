Customer relation management refers to the tools, technologies, and strategies businesses use to manage interactions with current and potential customers to improve relationships, increase customer satisfaction, and maximize profitability.

**Evolution**

| Era       | Description                                                |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1960s–70s | Manual customer records and personal selling               |
| 1980s     | Database Marketing (customer information stored digitally) |
| 1990s     | CRM software (Salesforce Automation)                       |
| 2000s     | Web-based CRM and cloud computing                          |
| 2010s     | Social CRM and Mobile CRM                                  |
| Present   | AI, Machine Learning, Big Data, Predictive CRM             |


**Emergence of CRM**

CRM emerged because businesses realized:
- Acquiring customers is expensive.
- Retaining customers is cheaper.
- Competition increased.
- Customers demanded personalized services.
- Information Technology made customer data easier to manage.

**Growth Factors of CRM**

Major factors responsible for CRM growth:
- Increased competition
- Internet and e-commerce
- Customer awareness
- Data analytics
- Mobile technology
- Social media
- Cloud computing
- Artificial Intelligence
- Globalization


**CRM Process**

```
Identify Customers
        ↓
Acquire Customers
        ↓
Understand Customer Needs
        ↓
Deliver Value
        ↓
Maintain Relationship
        ↓
Retain Customers
        ↓
Increase Loyalty & Profitability
```
