**Emergence of CRM**

CRM emerged because businesses realized:
- Acquiring customers is expensive.
- Retaining customers is cheaper.
- Competition increased.
- Customers demanded personalized services.
- Information Technology made customer data easier to manage.

**Growth Factors of CRM**

Major factors responsible for CRM growth:
- Increased competition
- Internet and e-commerce
- Customer awareness
- Data analytics
- Mobile technology
- Social media
- Cloud computing
- Artificial Intelligence
- Globalization

**Types of CRM**

A. Operational CRM

Focuses on daily operations.

Includes:

- Sales Automation
- Marketing Automation
- Customer Service Automation

Example:  
Customer support system.

B. Analytical CRM

Uses customer data for analysis.

Functions:

- Customer segmentation
- Predictive analysis
- Sales forecasting
- Profitability analysis

Example:  
Amazon recommendations.


C. Collaborative CRM

Improves communication among departments.

Departments:

- Sales
- Marketing
- Customer Support

Purpose:  
Provide consistent customer experience.



D. Strategic CRM

Focuses on long-term customer relationships.

Objective:

- Customer loyalty
- Customer lifetime value
- Long-term profitability

**Customer Profitability**

Customer Profitability measures how much profit a company earns from a customer.

Formula
```
Customer Profitability = Revenue − Cost of Serving Customer
```

Example
	
	Revenue = ₹50,000
	
	Service Cost = ₹30,000
	
	Profit = ₹20,000

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

	Profit expected from a customer over the entire relationship.
	
	Higher CLV = Better customer.
