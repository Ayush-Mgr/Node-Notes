# 1. CRM Planning Steps

**Definition:**  
CRM Planning is the process of designing and implementing a CRM strategy to build strong customer relationships and achieve business goals.

### Steps

```text
Set Business Goals
        ↓
Understand Customers
        ↓
Collect Customer Data
        ↓
Develop CRM Strategy
        ↓
Implement CRM System
        ↓
Monitor Performance
        ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

### Explanation

1. **Set Objectives** – Define what the business wants to achieve.
    
2. **Identify Customers** – Understand target customers.
    
3. **Collect Customer Data** – Gather customer information.
    
4. **Develop CRM Strategy** – Plan marketing, sales, and service activities.
    
5. **Implement CRM** – Use CRM software and train employees.
    
6. **Monitor Results** – Measure customer satisfaction and sales.
    
7. **Improve Continuously** – Update strategies based on feedback.

# 2. Customer Centricity

**Definition:**  
Customer Centricity is a business approach where all decisions are made by focusing on customer needs and satisfaction.

### Characteristics

- Customer-first approach
    
- Personalized services
    
- Long-term relationships
    
- Quick problem resolution
    
- Continuous feedback
    

### Benefits

- Higher customer satisfaction
    
- Increased loyalty
    
- Better retention
    
- Increased profits

# 8. Customer Strategy Grid

The **Customer Strategy Grid** helps businesses decide how to manage different customer groups based on **customer value** and **relationship strength**.

### Grid

|Relationship Strength|High Customer Value|Low Customer Value|
|---|---|---|
|**Strong Relationship**|Retain and Reward|Maintain|
|**Weak Relationship**|Develop Relationship|Evaluate or Reduce Investment|

### Explanation

#### High Value + Strong Relationship

- Most profitable customers.
    
- Offer loyalty programs and premium service.
    

#### High Value + Weak Relationship

- Customers have potential but are not yet loyal.
    
- Build trust through personalized offers and better engagement.
    

#### Low Value + Strong Relationship

- Existing loyal customers with lower spending.
    
- Maintain the relationship cost-effectively.
    

#### Low Value + Weak Relationship

- Least profitable segment.
    
- Minimize marketing costs or focus resources elsewhere.
